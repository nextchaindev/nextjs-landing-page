# Nextchain.site

register: brand

## Product purpose

Marketing site that sells Nextchain's website services to Vietnamese small businesses.

- **Core offer:** landing page + small-business website sold as a monthly plan, Durable-style. Gói Khởi đầu 249.000đ/tháng (~$10), Tăng trưởng 790.000đ, Chuyên nghiệp 1.490.000đ. No setup fee. Customer uses a free `ten-cua-ban.nextchain.site` address or connects their own domain.
- **Larger services** (project pricing Basic/Premium/Professional): Website Doanh nghiệp (từ 12tr), Website Bán hàng (từ 18tr), Thiết kế theo yêu cầu (từ 20tr), Marketing & SEO (từ 8tr), Phát triển phần mềm (từ 20tr).
- Operated by Nextchain Tech, Hồ Chí Minh. Contact contact@nextchain.kr, +84-909-807-687.

## Users

Owners of spa, salon, nail, homestay, café, small shops in Vietnam. Busy, mobile-first (most visits on phone, often from Facebook/Zalo/Google), not technical, price-sensitive, skeptical of agencies. They want to know in seconds: what do I get, how much, do I have to do anything myself.

## Brand voice

Warm, plain-spoken, trustworthy. "Chúng tôi làm từ A–Z, bạn chỉ cần duyệt." Vietnamese only, no English marketing jargon (no "Done-for-You"). Concrete and specific over hype.

## Hard rules

- Never mention AI in the production process. Nextchain's team prepares designs and content.
- No fabricated stats, testimonials, or ranking promises. Old "50+ dự án / 98% hài lòng / tăng 200%" claims were removed on purpose.
- Keep SEO: server-rendered content, real `<a href>` links, unique metadata per page, JSON-LD.
- Templates gallery is reference only ("Mẫu tham khảo"); every site is custom.

## Visual identity (keep)

The owner likes the original UI and wants it kept: orange brand `#F54900`, Be Vietnam Pro, light theme, real project imagery (project gallery), soft orange/blue blurred blobs, rounded cards with lift-on-hover, motion fade-up reveals, tabbed pricing cards with "Phổ biến nhất" gradient badge.

## Anti-references

Generic AI SaaS landing pages; dark neon tech; cream editorial magazines; stock-photo agency sites that promise "top Google".
